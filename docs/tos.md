## 1. What Orbit Is

Orbit helps college students discover activities ("missions"), form small
groups ("pods"), coordinate a time to meet, and chat with their group.
Missions may have a fixed time chosen by their creator, or may start as a
scheduling activity where the group shares availability and confirms a time
together. Either way, the end result is a scheduled, in-person activity.

Orbit is a coordination tool. **We do not organize, host, supervise, or
endorse any activity arranged through the Service**, and we are not a party
to any interaction between users.

## 4. Data We Collect and How We Use It

This section summarizes our data practices; the full detail lives in the
[Privacy Policy](privacy.md). We collect:

**a. Profile information you give us.** Your name, `.edu` email, campus,
class year, interests, profile and gallery photos, bio, links, and any
optional fields you choose to fill in (e.g. gender, MBTI).

**b. Activity and interaction data.** What missions you view, join, skip,
and leave; whether you attended; your availability entries when scheduling;
post-activity survey answers (e.g. enjoyment ratings and peer feedback);
pod membership; friend connections; and messages you send in pod chats and
direct messages.

**c. Usage data.** App opens, session identifiers, feature usage events
(e.g. viewing a mission, opening a notification), how long you engage with
parts of the app, your platform, and app version. Where feasible this data
is stored under a **pseudonymous identifier** rather than your name or
email.

**d. Approximate location — campus only.** We use the campus you select to
show nearby activities. **We do not collect GPS or precise device
location.**

**We use this data to:**

1. Operate the Service (accounts, missions, pods, chat, notifications).
2. **Personalize recommendations.** Orbit scores activities for you using
   your interests, your campus, and your behavioral history — for example,
   the kinds of events you join or leave, what you rated highly, and tags
   of activities you interacted with. This is the core of the product and
   the main reason we collect interaction data.
3. **Keep the community reliable.** Attendance confirmations and peer
   survey feedback feed a reliability ("trust") score that influences how
   activities and users are ranked. This is automated processing; it never
   makes a decision with legal effect, and you can contact us to contest a
   score you believe is wrong.
4. Moderate content, investigate abuse, and enforce these Terms.
5. Debug, measure, and improve the Service using aggregate or pseudonymous
   analytics.

**What we don't do:**

- We do **not sell** your personal information.
- We do **not** share your personal information with advertisers.
- We do **not** use the contents of your private messages for
  recommendations or advertising. Message content may be reviewed only in
  response to a report, for moderation, or as required by law.

**Service providers.** We host data with Google Cloud Platform (App Engine,
Datastore, Cloud Storage) in the United States. Providers process data only
on our behalf.

**Retention and deletion.** Expired missions, pods, and their chats are
deleted automatically on a rolling basis. If you delete your account, we
delete your profile, your missions, your pod memberships, your friend
connections, and your analytics events. Residual copies may persist in
backups for a limited period. Messages you sent in group chats may remain
visible to the group without your name attached where removal is
technically infeasible. [VERIFY this last sentence against actual chat
